Which preset fits your PC?

General examples — base your choice on your actual hardware, not just the label.

  • 4–8GB RAM
  • Integrated graphics
  • Older / lower-power CPUs

Start with the Potato preset.

Mid-range

  • 8–16GB RAM
  • GTX / RX class GPUs
