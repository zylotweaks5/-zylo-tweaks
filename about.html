ABOUT

Why ZYLO TWEAKS exists

Started because Fortnite ran badly on a normal PC, and most guides online either did nothing or asked you to run something you couldn't verify.

What this actually is

ZYLO TWEAKS is a set of free, written guides for improving Fortnite performance on Windows — covering low-end hardware, AMD, Intel, and Nvidia setups, network settings, and general Windows tuning. Every guide is plain steps you follow yourself, in Fortnite's own settings menu or Windows Settings.

No downloads. No scripts. No admin rights required. Anything that changes settings on its own has to run as a program on your PC, and that comes with real risk if you don't know exactly what it's doing. Keeping everything as guides means you can see the change before you make it, and undo it just as easily.

What it isn't

  • No cheats, ESP, aimbots, or anti-cheat bypasses
  • No guaranteed FPS numbers — results depend on your own hardware
  • No paid tiers that don't exist yet, and no fake "Pro" upgrade

What's next

More guides get added as they're written and tested. The Tracker page lets you check off what you've applied so far, and the Discord is the place to ask questions or suggest what to cover next.
