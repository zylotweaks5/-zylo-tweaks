Why a checklist instead of a one-click tool? Anything that actually changes Windows settings, the registry, or game files on its own needs to run as a program on your PC — and that comes with real risk if you don't fully trust where it came from. ZYLO TWEAKS keeps everything as plain instructions you can read, understand, and reverse yourself. Slower, but nothing hidden.
